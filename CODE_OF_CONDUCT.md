# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct v2.1

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity,
experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

**Positive behaviour:** empathy, respect, constructive feedback, accountability.
**Unacceptable:** harassment, insults, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing.

Report incidents to **conduct@mcpguard.dev**. All complaints reviewed promptly.
